DESIGN DETAILS

Design of the proposed system is geared towards establishing a scalable, real-time, and multilingual pipeline for crisis monitoring and intelligent routing of public needs. Event-driven and layered approach will be utilized, which includes the process of data collection, pre-processing, analysis, and human validation. Contrary to typical systems where sentiment analysis alone is performed, the designed system incorporates several layers, including need recognition, fake news risk, priority, and situation level summarization to increase decision-making efficiency. The use of human in the loop allows increasing confidence and avoiding false positive outcomes that may result from full automation. Such an approach allows handling of large amounts of data and their processing in real-time to produce useful output.
4.1 Novelty
Taking advantage of these innovations, the design of the system is not only focused on functionality but more importantly, effectiveness in practice as well. Intelligence processing and scalability architecture have been incorporated into the design process in order to achieve precise, prompt, and reliable results. The succeeding sub-sections elaborate on these three features of the design.
4.1.1 Multilingual + code-mixed handling
The fundamental innovation of the suggested system is its capability of surpassing conventional sentiment analysis by integrating multilingualism, need discovery, instantaneous execution, and intelligence in routing all in one. The distinction between the current system and previous ones which have mainly concentrated on sentiment categorization for the English language alone is that the proposed system is equipped to manage multilingualism and code-mixing, especially in places like India, where individuals tend to speak different languages, including Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, and English in a single interaction.
 
4.1.2 Sentiment + need detection combination
Yet another important development in this regard is the combination of the sentiment analysis process with the need detection process. The system will no longer analyze the post for its sentiment but try to understand the actual reason for the expression of that sentiment by the user. For instance, if the post is about healthcare, law enforcement, municipal affairs, or tourism needs, the system can analyze that too.
4.1.3 Real-time crisis monitoring
Real time crisis monitoring has also been put into consideration using event-driven analysis to ensure that any crisis occurring is detected in good time. This is achieved by analyzing the social media channels in real time and not through delayed batch processing techniques. The real time processing aspect is important when it comes to crisis management situations.
4.1.4 Departmental routing
Another distinguishing feature of the process is the automatic departmental routing of the requirements that have been identified. In doing this, social media data is converted from an unstructured to a structured form that can be used to inform decisions.
4.2 Innovativeness
4.2.1 Fake news hybrid scoring
The system includes some technological innovations that contribute greatly to making the process of crisis monitoring more efficient than the traditional systems. One of the innovations is the hybrid method of fake news detection, which does not depend exclusively on the machine learning algorithm but employs various criteria for assessing the reliability of the information received. The fake score is calculated based on the following formula: machine learning algorithm result (50%), inverted score of credibility (30%), and content risks (20%). Credibility refers to the age of the account, number of   followers and other criteria, while content risks refer to hyperboles and other aspects that may signal  
deception.
4.2.2 Priority scoring mechanism
The other significant contribution of the project is the priority scoring system, where the discovered situation is ranked in terms of its priority. The algorithm does not treat every alert as equal but scores them in order of their priority depending on various parameters. These include need urgency (50%), intensity of the sentiment (20%), severity of the keyword used (15%), and recency (10%).
4.2.3 Situation-level summarization
The process of innovation is escalated by means of summarization according to the situation. Conventional systems generate several separate alerts, making it difficult to grasp the situation entirely. This current system differs from traditional methods as it relies on transformers for categorizing relevant posts and then summarizing them in a comprehensible manner.
4.2.4 Evidence extraction using embeddings
Moreover, for evidence extraction, embedding based similarity methods have been employed. The posts from social media have been represented in vector form and then clustered using similarity metrics. Further ranking is carried out on those relevant posts by applying a scoring function which considers semantic similarity and prioritization.
4.3 Performance
The layered and event-driven architecture of the system provides efficient processing of large amounts of social media data. The layer of data ingestion is responsible for real-time streaming with lightweight pipeline and queue management, thus providing near real-time responsiveness for crisis monitoring purposes.
Preprocessing and features extraction make use of transformer models, such as XLM-R, for language and emotion detection and need recognition through the use of tokenization limiters, batching, and pre- 
trained models. Modular construction allows smooth running of the tasks including sentiment analysis, need detection, fake news evaluation, and summary creation which leads to reduction in processing times. Prioritization and filtering techniques allow for efficient allocation of computing resources towards handling important posts. Efficient structured database design ensures rapid storage and retrieval of information for dashboard display.
4.4 Reliability
System reliability is achieved via several design features that will eliminate any error and increase the efficiency and reliability of the system in practice. The first and foremost of the features to consider here is related to dealing with the false positive issues by employing a hybrid algorithm for detecting fake news. As opposed to the model prediction alone, the system uses an assessment based on the model-based probability of post being false, along with its credibility and danger level.
One essential characteristic of the technology involves having a human validation level in place. Important inputs such as situational summaries and alerts would be validated by humans before being accepted as outputs. This would ensure that there is no mistake when an automated decision-making process is involved, and it would address the problem of sarcasm and other difficult situations. Another characteristic of the technology includes having scoring methods in place. The scores obtained from the algorithm can be used to determine which outputs should be focused on by decision makers. This would ensure that decision makers would only focus on information that they can trust.
4.5 Maintainability
Maintainability has been the cornerstone of the system design, and it has largely been made possible by the layered architecture employed. All functional aspects of the system such as data input, processing, application of algorithms, storage, and dashboards have been designed separately as modules, ensuring that all layers operate independently of each other. The separation makes debugging and updating much easier since problems can be pinpointed in particular layers without impacting the whole pipeline process. For example, any problems with preprocessing or with the model itself would not require changes to the ingestion layer or the dashboard layer. 
Moreover, the architecture facilitates the possibility of replacing or upgrading the model. As machine learning parts are independent of the whole system, new or improved models (such as the latest version of transformers) can easily be incorporated into the entire process with very little adjustment required.
Lastly, the explicit segregation of codes for data ingestion, logic, machine learning, and user interface contributes to an organized coding practice and enhances clarity within the development team.
4.6 Security
The system has been built in such a way as to ensure that the security of basic data has been ensured since the entire system operates using only publicly available social media data. No personally identifiable information has been collected and used within the system to mitigate any risk of violating personal privacy. The fact that the data being analyzed is already publicly available means that there will be no problem with data confidentiality and other stringent data protection laws.
4.7 Reusability
High reusability of the system is guaranteed by the fact that its structure is modular and the processing pipeline is quite general. Models for sentiment analysis and need detection that were created in the process of training can be easily transferred to another case study where there is a need for, say, analyzing customers' opinions about a product or public mood analysis on any topic. Moreover, the pipeline is flexible enough to easily integrate data from a new source without redesigning the whole system. 
CHAPTER V

HIGH LEVEL SYSTEM DESIGN / SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
The architecture of the system has been developed using an event-driven, real-time approach based on the pipeline architecture that is used for transforming continuous input streams of data into useful information. The architecture facilitates separation of concerns and maintains scalability and maintainability in its design approach. In this model, the system is based on event-oriented design, whereby social media posts received by the system act as events that start off the processing chain. This provides a stream of continuous data, with analysis done in real time and without having to wait for batch processing. Moreover, since the system works in real time, it will ensure that all crises that arise are identified immediately, which can be important when taking action.

5.2 Data Ingestion Layer
The function of this layer is to collect information from the social media or simulated social media feeds. Metadata such as the date and source identifier will be tagged onto each posting received. In order to ensure that there are no data loss in the system, the data ingestion process is designed to accommodate an ongoing stream of data.
5.3 Preprocessing and Analysis Layer
It is the task of this layer to gather the information that comes from social media channels or from simulated streams. The posts received are tagged with appropriate metadata including time and ID of the source. This ingestion process has been made to deal with streaming data effectively and avoid missing any event that may come in.
5.4 Machine Learning Pipeline, Result and Human review Interface
The preprocessing stage helps to clean and format the raw text from social media before feeding it to models. This is achieved by removing any type of noise, which may include URLs, hashtags, mention tags, among others, and normalizing the text. The language detection mechanism detects the language of posts; this ensures that there are adequate inputs fed into the subsequent layers.
There is also a stage that performs the main intelligence of the architecture through various mechanisms as shown below: Sentiment detection - This is where the classifier classifies posts on the basis of sentiment types (positive, negative, or neutral). Need detection – Detects need from the post (e.g., healthcare, police, government services, tourism, etc.). Fake news scoring – Score based on credibility analysis using an integrated method that combines predictions from models, sources, and risk levels. Summarization mechanism – Summary formation to reduce noise. Evidence extraction – Ranking of posts through the use of similarity embedding. Prior to the use of any final products, there is a step in the process whereby a human validates the output. The reviewers may confirm, approve, or even reject the summary or alert produced by the system.
 
5.5 Dash-board and UI interface layer
The dashboard offers an interactive platform for tracking detected situations, alerts, and system outputs. Prioritized alerts, summaries, trends, and regional analysis, like heat maps, are shown on this layer. The goal of this layer is to provide a platform for decision-makers to grasp the situation and take relevant action
On the basis of detected requirements, the system automatically channels each situation towards the corresponding department, such as health, law, municipality, or tourism sectors. Hence, no human involvement is needed in providing information to the right department.
Priority scoring is a way of prioritizing different situations depending on the severity, emotional weightage, and relevance of the event. Alongside with this process, the system also produces outputs in form of situation report and trend analysis daily.
